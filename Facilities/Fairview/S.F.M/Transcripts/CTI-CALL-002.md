**Participants:**
- Marcus Hale, Senior Research Director, Citrus Technologies Inc.

[LINE CONNECTED — 02:06 PM]

**HALE:** Mr. Prime Minister. Right on time.

**BATISTA:** Marcus. I've got the preliminary data in front of me but I'd rather hear it from you. How did it go?

**HALE:** Clean. Twenty-two days on Fairview's municipal grid, no reactor faults. NFI stayed between 1.2 and 3.8 the entire run — never touched the SCRAM threshold. Coolant temperature held steady. Cladding integrity across all twenty rods is sitting at 97% average as of this morning.

**BATISTA:** Any incidents at all?

**HALE:** One. Day eleven, Cluster C output dropped unexpectedly during the evening shift. Took about forty minutes to diagnose.

**BATISTA:** Root cause?

**CALLOWAY:** Operator error. The technician on shift manually adjusted rod spacing on C3 without logging it first and bumped it past the upper threshold. Reaction on that rod idled down and dragged the cluster output with it. Once we identified it we corrected the spacing and Cluster C was back to normal within eight minutes.

**BATISTA:** So the reactor caught itself.

**CALLOWAY:** Essentially. C3 didn't fault — it just slowed down the way it's supposed to when spacing is too wide. The system did exactly what it was designed to do. The problem was the technician not following procedure, not the x20.

**BATISTA:** And outside of that?

**HALE:** Nothing. Grid draw peaked at around 84% of Fairview's total load on day seventeen during a facility-wide equipment test they had scheduled. The x20 handled it without any adjustment from the control room. Clusters stayed stable, NFI barely moved.

**BATISTA:** *(pause)* Alright. I've heard enough.

**HALE:** And?

**BATISTA:** Parafield formally declares the S.F.M x20 project a success. You're cleared to proceed however Citrus sees fit — further development, scaling, whatever direction you want to take it. We'll document this as a passed evaluation and file it under the NatElec agreement.

**HALE:** That means a lot. Genuinely. The team has put a lot into this.

**BATISTA:** Tell Calloway she called it.

**HALE:** *(laughs)* She's right here and she's already insufferable about it.

**CALLOWAY:** *(in background)* I heard that.

**BATISTA:** One thing I'll say for the record — Parafield will be backing this going forward. Funding included. Whatever Citrus needs to develop this further, we want to support it properly rather than leave you scraping for resources.

**HALE:** That's... not something we were expecting. Thank you.

**BATISTA:** I'll also be talking with National Electrodynamics. If there's anything they can provide on top of what we're putting in — additional support, materials access, whatever's useful — I'll make sure that conversation happens. You shouldn't have to chase any of that down yourself.

**HALE:** Wouldn't have it any other way. You're the reason any of this exists.

**BATISTA:** Citrus did the work Marcus. We just gave you the material. *(pause)* Good work. Both of you. Pass it along to the rest of the team.

**HALE:** Will do. Thank you, sir.
